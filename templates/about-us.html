About Rido

Smarter travel through shared rides 🚗

What is Rido?

Rido is a web-based carpooling platform that connects drivers with available seats to passengers travelling in the same direction. By encouraging ride sharing, Rido helps reduce travel costs, traffic congestion, and environmental pollution while making everyday commuting more convenient and efficient.

Our Purpose

🚗 Convenience

Easily search, post, and manage rides through a simple, user-friendly platform.

💰 Affordability

Share fuel expenses with fellow travellers and reduce transportation costs.

🌱 Sustainability

Help reduce traffic congestion and lower carbon emissions by promoting shared travel.

How It Works

1️⃣ Search Ride

Passengers can quickly search for rides matching their destination.

2️⃣ Post Ride

Drivers can publish available rides with travel details, seats, and fare.

3️⃣ Book & Travel

Send a booking request, receive driver approval, and enjoy a safe shared journey.

Why Choose Rido?

Rido focuses on simplicity, transparency, and efficiency. With secure authentication, ride management, booking requests, and an intuitive interface, Rido makes everyday travel smarter, more economical, and environmentally responsible.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and smart ride-sharing community that makes daily travel affordable, sustainable, and accessible while encouraging environmentally friendly transportation.
